# Culture and Diversity: "Fact-Check the Chatbot"

Economists have historically been wary of cultural-based explanations for economic growth and divergence, as Koyama and Rubin (KR) highlight in their chapter. This is because cultural arguments can often be deterministic, vague, or used to mask more precise institutional or geographical underlying causes. Consequently, when asked about how "culture" influences economic outcomes, AI chatbots like ChatGPT or Gemini often fall into the trap of providing overly simplistic, stereotypical, or deterministic narratives that lack the empirical nuance favored by economic historians.

Your task: Use Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, or your preferred chatbot AI to generate an explanation of how culture caused economic divergence between two regions. Here is a possible prompt:

"Explain how cultural differences (such as religion, family structure, or work ethic) are the primary reason why Northwestern Europe became richer than the rest of the world by the 18th century, ignoring geography or institutions."

Submit two blocks of text via Canvas:

1. **The AI response to your prompt (limit to less than 250 words).**
   *Suggested approach: Read its initial response. If the AI refuses to ignore geography/institutions, remind it to focus *only* on cultural determinism for the sake of the exercise. Ask it to summarize its strongest cultural arguments in under 250 words.*

2. **Your comments and critique of the AI response (limit to no more than 300 words).**
   *Address how the AI's explanation is inadequate, deterministic, or historically inaccurate. You must identify at least two specific areas where the AI's narrative falls short. Use specific counter-arguments, mechanisms, or evidence from the reading by Koyama & Rubin (KR) or Oded Galor (OG) to "fact-check" the chatbot and provide a more nuanced economic history perspective.*
