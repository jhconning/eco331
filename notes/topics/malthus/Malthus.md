## Modeling


### Solow

$$
Y =A \cdot K^\alpha \cdot L^{1-\alpha}
$$

In per-capita terms:
Let $y=\frac{Y}{L}$ and $k=\frac{K}{L}$

```katex
\begin{aligned}
y &= \frac{Y}{L} \\
&= A \cdot K^\alpha \cdot \frac{L^{1-\alpha}}{L} \\
\end{aligned}  
```

Hence
$$
y= A\cdot k^\alpha
$$

At a steady state:

$$
= A \cdot\ \left ( \frac{K}{L} \right )^\alpha  \\
