## Indicative Grades and 'the curve'

On gradescope take note of two scores which I will call **mc** and **es**. The first is your Total Points on the bubble sheet (e.g. out of 15 points) and the second is your score on the essay section of the exam (e.g. out of 60 points). You will also be able to find scanned versions of your exam with my scoring and written comments.

The exam is 'curved' which means that I have combined the **mc** and **es** scores to get to a total score, **ts** in such a way to reshape the distribution of raw scores to match some broadly defined targets, based on past performance on these exams. The (very generous) formula that I used was: 

ts= es + mc *2 + 10

In words: everyone gets 10 points just for showing up and then 2 points for each correct multiple choice plus your essays score. This formula is designed to ‘curve’ the distribution of grades. It means that scores will range from a potential minimum of 10 points up to a potential maximum of 100 points for a perfect exam. The formula favors lower raw scores (since they get a larger percentage boost with those 10 points).
