# Galor summaries

Galor, O., 2022. _The Journey of Humanity: The Origins of Wealth and Inequality_. Penguin.



ISBN: 978-0593185995

## Big questions

- What are the roots of the dramatic transformation in living standards in past centuries after hundreds of thousands of years of stagnation?

- Why is the world so unequal?


## Mysteries of the Human Journey

- Hobbes "continual fear, and danger of violent death; and the life of man, solitary, poor, nasty, brutish, and short’ (Leviathan 1651, i. xiii. 9).
  - 25% of new borns died before their 1st birthday
  - Life expectancy rarely exceeded 40.
  - Darkness after sunset
  - long hours just to get water, washed infrequently. Smoke filled homes.
  - A crisis nnot just belt-tightening but mass starvation and death. 
  - On Hobbes: earliest social contract theory

![](Clipboard_2023-01-30-15-46-32.png =150x)

- Wrong prevailing wisdom that living standards have been rising incrementally...A distortion.
  - Evolution of technology has been gradual, accelerating over time
  - But did NOT result in corresponding improvement in living conditions.
  - Astounding ascent in quality of life has in fact been abrupt transformation.
