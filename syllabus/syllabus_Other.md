Here is a possible syllabus for a course on economic history focused on the theme of how the world became rich:

Week 1: Introduction to economic history and the theme of the course

-   Definition and scope of economic history
-   The importance of economic growth for human well-being
-   Key questions and debates in the study of economic growth

Week 2: The pre-modern world

-   The economic systems of ancient civilizations
-   The rise and fall of empires
-   The role of religion and culture in shaping economic systems

Week 3: The emergence of capitalism

-   The origins of capitalism in Europe
-   The Industrial Revolution and its impact on economic growth
-   The role of institutions in shaping economic development

Week 4: Geography and economic growth

-   The role of geography in shaping economic systems
-   The impact of natural resources on economic development
-   The role of transportation and communication in economic growth

Week 5: The rise of the modern corporation

-   The history of the corporation and its impact on economic growth
-   The role of the state in shaping corporate development
-   The global expansion of the corporation

Week 6: Slavery and economic growth

-   The role of slavery in the development of capitalist economies
-   The impact of slavery on economic growth in the United States and other countries
-   The abolition of slavery and its impact on economic growth

Week 7: Class struggle and economic growth

-   The role of class conflict in shaping economic systems
-   The impact of labor movements on economic growth
-   The role of unions in shaping economic development

Week 8: Colonization and economic growth

-   The economic consequences of colonization for colonized and colonizing countries
-   The impact of colonialism on economic growth in the developing world
-   The legacy of colonization in the modern global economy

Week 9: Culture and economic growth

-   The role of culture in shaping economic systems
-   The impact of cultural values on economic development
-   The relationship between culture and economic growth

Week 10: Review and conclusion

-   Recap of key themes and debates
-   Implications for understanding the modern global economy
-   Future directions for research in economic history
