# The Logic of Bandits

**Eco 330: Economic History**
Mancur Olson's Theory of State Formation

---

# The Roving Bandit

In a world of anarchy, the **Roving Bandit** has a short time horizon.

* **Incentive:** To steal everything and move on.
* **Destruction:** Destroys the incentive for victims to invest or produce.
* **Result:** Total output in the economy is low because production is discouraged.

> "Under anarchy, uncoordinated roving banditry destroys the incentive to invest and produce, leaving little for either the population or the bandits." — *Mancur Olson*

---

# The Stationary Bandit

When a bandit settles down to monopolize theft in a specific territory, they become a **Stationary Bandit**.

* **Encompassing Interest:** The bandit now cares about the future productivity of the victims.
* **Public Goods:** Provides protection and order to maximize taxable income.
* **Taxation:** Optimizes the theft rate (taxes) so victims still have an incentive to produce.

# From Banditry to Civilization

The shift from roving to stationary banditry is the **origin of the state**.

* It replaces anarchy with **autocracy**.
* The autocrat provides peaceful order not out of benevolence, but to maximize revenue.
* This "invisible hand" leads the rational self-interested leader to provide public goods that benefit the population.

> The rational stationary bandit will take only a part of income in taxes, because he will be able to exact a larger total amount of income from his subjects if he leaves them with an incentive to generate income.
