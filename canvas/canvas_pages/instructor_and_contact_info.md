## Jonathan Conning

## Office Hours

Tuesdays and Fridays 4–5pm and by appointment at Hunter West 1504a. Email: [jconning@hunter.cuny.edu](mailto:jconning@hunter.cuny.edu) (please add "Eco 331:" at the start of your subject heading).

**About myself:** I have taught at Hunter College and The Graduate Center since 2002 after teaching at Williams College in Massachussetts for several years. I've also held visiting or adjunct teaching positions at The University of Chile, Yale, Ozaka University, Columbia, NYU, and The University of Cape Town, South Africa. My research and teaching interests involve applying micro-economic theory to better understand economic history, development economics, and political economy. My current esearch includes a focus on the evolution of land property rights regimes and land and labor market institutions around the world. I grew up in Chile and still have strong ties to the country through family, friends, and research. In my free time, I enjoy traveling, hiking, reading about economic history, and coding. My PhD in Economics is from Yale University.
