### Agricultural transformation 
- Deep Agrarian reforms in Japan, Taiwan, Korea.  
  Raised agricultural productivity, equalized ownership, releasted resources. Created demand for new products and services.
- Not enough by itself.
### Why need Manufacturing in takeoff strategy?
* * Poor countries need to overcome limitations in productive human skills. 
* Manufacturing offers potential for faster productivity gains compared to services. 
* Manufactured goods are more easily traded internationally than services.

* "Any nation which by means of protective duties and restrictions on navigation has raised her manufacturing power and her navigation to such a degree of development that no other nation can sustain free competition with her can do nothing wiser than to throw away these ladders of her greatness, to preach to other nations the benefits of free trade, and to declare in penitent tones that she has hitherto wandered in the paths of error, and has now for the first time succeeded in discovering the truth."

### State Intervention in Manufacturing
* Governments try to nurture manufacturing through protection and subsidies. 
* **To Repair Market Failure:**
	*  "breathing space" for entrepreneurs to develop competitive manufacturing capabilities.
	* Argument for subsidy must rest on market failures:
		* knowledge spillovers across firms
		* credit market failures
		* coordination failures and big push

- **The Peril of Rent-Seeking**
	* when entrepreneurs focus on securing benefits from the state instead of enhancing technological progress and competitiveness. 
	* The challenge is to align the interests of businesses with national development by compelling firms to compete globally. 


---

### Export Discipline as a Solution
* Successful East Asian economies implemented "export discipline" (outward orientation). 
* Required manufacturers to export as a condition for receiving support. Export performance as metric to assess the merit of state support. 
* Helps governments determine whether nurtured industries are meeting global standards.
