## Open Field
- Before 1500 most land in England and N Europe farmed under open fields.
- Village typically managed land communally:
    - 3 large fields (rotation system). Each field divided into large number of strips of land. 
    - Meadow, Pasture, Woodlands, and 'wastes'  

- Animals grazed on these commons as well as on fallow 
- Meant all farmers in village had to follow the same crop rotation and schedule. (McCloskey, 1972; Allen )
