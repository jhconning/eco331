Paper presentation assignments

Here is the table of student paper presentation assignments. The corresponding numbered list of papers can be seen after the table.

Most papers are assigned to groups of 4, but in one case a group of 5. I tried to accommodate your preferences, but some of you may have ended up with a 2nd, 3rd, or 4th choice, and in one or two cases to a paper you did not choose (but that you will find interesting ;). You may ‘swap’ with a classmate, but if you do please let me know in advance. The presentation dates will be slightly moving targets, but they will present more or less in the order you see here, and you’ll get at least 2 weeks notice. Check the reading schedule for presentation dates.
