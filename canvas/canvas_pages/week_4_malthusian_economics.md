# Week 4: Malthusian Economics

## Overview

This week we explore Malthus's theory of population and economic stagnation.

## Readings

  * OG 2 "Lost in Stagnation" (pp. 27-41)
  * OG 3 "The Storm Beneath the Surface" (pp. 43-55)



## Key Concepts

Concept | Description  
---|---  
Positive checks | Factors that increase death rate (famine, disease, war)  
Preventive checks | Factors that reduce birth rate (delayed marriage, celibacy)  
Subsistence wage | The wage level at which population stabilizes  
  
## Discussion Questions

  1. Why did living standards remain stagnant for so long?
  2. What finally allowed escape from the Malthusian trap?
