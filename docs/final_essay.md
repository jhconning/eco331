Throughout the course we have discussed prominent explanations for the difference in comparative economic and political performance across countries and regions. In Koyama and Rubin's classification these include:

a) Geography <br>
b) Institutions <br>
c) Culture <br>
d) Fewer babies <br>
e) Colonization and Exploitation

**Your Task:**
Using this scheme, choose a region or country, in any historical period, and highlight one or two key determinants of its relative success or lack of success (based on this list or otherwise). Provide some evidence to support your arguments. 

*Note:* Try to avoid pointing to possible explanations without explaining how they came about. For example, it's not enough to say "the country stayed poor because of lack of centralized state capacity and corruption" ... those may very well be factors, but your challenge is to try to explain why that happened in your case, and why you think it's causal. You can do this bv pointing to historical events or by contrasting with other countries or regions.

**Length:** keep it short and focused.  Four or five paragraphs will suffice.
